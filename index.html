Welcome to Acinima!

This webpage is designed to provide an accessible and simple to use service for booking movies, tracking new releases and bookmarking potential future watches.

Using our services, you can:

  • View movies currently airing
  • View recommendations tailored to you (account required)
  • View, create and manage your account
  • Book tickets to movies currently airing and upcoming
  • Add movies to a list of titles to watch in the future
